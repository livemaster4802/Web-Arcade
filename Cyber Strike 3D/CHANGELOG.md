# 🚀 Cyber Strike 3D — Patch v1.1.0 (Tactical Link Update)

Welcome to the latest update for **Cyber Strike 3D**! This patch focuses on combat responsiveness, local multiplayer stability, and smoother mobile landscape support.

---

### ✨ New Features & Systems
* **Local Multiplayer Rooms:** Added browser-tab LAN matchmaking with custom Callsigns and shareable Room Codes (`CYBER-01`).
* **Commander Upgrades:** Added permanent combat enhancements that save automatically to your device using earned mission credits.
* **Gun Shop & Skins:** Introduced a 3-slot **Equipped Armory** loadout system so you can customize your weapons before deploying.

### ⚔️ Combat & Gameplay Balance
* **Precision Rewards:** Headshots now deal **2x critical damage** and grant bonus mission credits.
* **Wave Pacing:** Improved enemy spawn timing for rogue synth-bots across endless waves.
* **HUD & Tactical Alerts:** Added low-ammo reload prompts (`[R]`), grenade counters (`[G]`), and live combo multipliers (`COMBO x2`).

### 🛠️ UI, Controls & Bug Fixes
* **Mobile Landscape Support:** Added an automatic landscape orientation prompt and a **Continue Anyway** bypass for mobile browsers.
* **Instant Settings Auto-Save:** Tweaks in the Settings menu now apply immediately without requiring a restart, with a one-click **Reset Defaults** option.
* **Pause & Fail Screens:** Added quick-restart and direct Gun Shop access from the **System Failure** screen.
