# ⚡ Cyber Arena — Patch v1.0.0 (Neon Combat Release)

Welcome to **Cyber Arena** by Volcan Studio—an ultra-optimized 2D circular bot battle royale built for smooth 60 FPS action on both desktop and mobile!

---

### ✨ New Features & Systems
* **Pre-Match Starting Powers:** Choose your spawn perk before entering the arena: **+HP** (+50 Max HP), **SHIELD** (50 Armor), **RAPID** (15s 2x Fire Rate), or **3X SHOT** (15s Triple Spread).
* **Armory Shop & Upgrades:** Spend mission credits earned from eliminations and survival time on permanent combat upgrades.
* **Daily Missions:** Added auto-refreshing daily objectives with local device progress saving for bonus credits.
* **Live Arena Events:** Added **Boss Waves**, **Risk Zones**, **500-Credit Bounties**, kill streaks, and a live **Top Fighters** leaderboard.

### ⚔️ Combat & Controls
* **Auto-Lock Targeting & Sprinting:** Weapons automatically lock onto the nearest bot in range. Holding Sprint (`Shift` or `SPRINT` button) boosts movement speed by **65%** at the cost of stamina.
* **Arena Pickups:** Added four tactical drops in combat: **+HP Medkit**, **+50 Shield Armor**, **2x Rapid Fire**, and **3X Spread Shot**.
* **Cross-Platform Support:** Full support for both desktop keyboard controls (`WASD`, `Shift`, `P`/`Esc`, `M`/`N`, `G`) and mobile touch-and-drag movement.

### 🛠️ Performance & Settings
* **Bot AI Difficulty Tiers:** Added **Recruit**, **Veteran**, and **Elite** AI settings to customize bot aggression and accuracy.
* **Graphics Performance Modes:** Added **Ultra Fast**, **Balanced**, and **High Detail** presets for smooth frame rates across older and modern devices.
* **Audio & Match Summary:** Added independent toggles for Sound Effects and Background Synth Music, plus post-match stats tracking **Final Score**, **Kills**, **Accuracy %**, and **Credits Earned**.
